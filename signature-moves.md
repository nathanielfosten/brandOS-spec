# Signature Moves

A signature move is a choice this brand makes repeatedly that someone could recognize before seeing the logo. It might be the way a photo is cropped, the question that opens an email, or a small interaction that makes someone smile.

Start here when making something for the brand. Use a move when it serves the brief. Record a real example and why the team would repeat it so another maker can apply it in a new context.

## Record a move

### [Name the move with a verb]

- **What to do** — Describe the action precisely enough for someone to try it.
- **When it fits** — Name the audience, situation or format where it works.
- **Why we use it** — Explain what this choice does for the audience and what makes it ours.
- **Example** — Link to approved work and point to the detail worth repeating.

## Illustrative example

**Put the product in someone’s hands.** When a product page introduces a physical control, show a hand using it. For a radio, that could mean a close crop of fingers turning the tuning dial. The image teaches the reader what they can do with the product. A dimensions diagram serves a different purpose and can use a straight-on view.
